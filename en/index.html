01 · What it does

Everything you used to do by hand, with the cost sheet next to it.

Vinted shows you the price. Stoqzy shows you what's left. From there, each tool does one concrete thing: restock, relist, reply, ship. The screenshots are real, from a test account with three items.

1.1

A wardrobe with a record per item

Cards or a table, whichever you prefer. On each listing you add a SKU, a cost and where it's stored. The real margin shows up next to the price, and CSV goes in and out whenever you want.

  • aEdit without leaving. Price, title and description. After saving, the extension reads the listing again and confirms that only what you asked for changed.
  • bHide and unhide in bulk, to pull whatever you don't want to sell this week out of your catalogue.
  • cPhotos with 12 styles and pixel touch-ups in the editor. Every relist goes out with one of 20 editing profiles (framing, tilt, mirror, colour) that item hasn't had yet, so the photo doesn't come out identical to last time.
1.2

Restocker: stock, stale, sold, prices

Vinted has no stock: once it sells, it's gone. Here you mark each product as “restock” and say how many units you have. When that product sells, the listing is created again, as soon as the sale shows up or only once it's completed, your choice, and the quantity drops by one. Relisting stale items is proven live; restocking sold ones uses the same path and is in testing on our own account.

  • 1Stock per product. SKU, restock, quantity N or unlimited.
  • 2Relist the stale ones. A new listing, and the old one is hidden in the same operation. Never two on sale at the same time.
  • 3Put the sold ones back. As soon as the sale shows up (sold today, listed today) or only once it's completed, your choice. A cancelled sale never creates a duplicate on its own: Vinted doesn't relist it, and the cancellation goes through you.
  • 4Slow price drops, with a floor. Cautious, Balanced and Active modes.

What it doesn't do: it doesn't delete listings (only what we've confirmed in real traffic gets built, and deleting isn't confirmed yet) and it doesn't relist in bursts. Every action joins the queue and waits its turn.

1.3

Messages, interested buyers and offers

Your conversations in a simple master and detail, saved replies so you don't type the same thing twenty times, and offers sent from the same window. In Interested you see who added each piece to their favourites, and with one click you send them a human message with the discount you choose.

Every message goes through the queue and the human pace. Before the queue existed, a test that fired messages one after another got our test account restricted. The mandatory pace was born right there, and since then every message waits its turn.

1.4

Figures: an honest portrait of the wardrobe

How much money is tied up, for how long, and which brands tie up the most. Vinted gives you no history, so Stoqzy doesn't draw fake trends: it shows the portrait of right now and lets you export the CSV to compare later.

It was by looking at these figures that we worked out, in our own wardrobe, that half the stock was sitting still and the prices were far too high. The Restocker and the price drops came from that.

02 · How it works

Three steps, and not one of them asks for your password.

You install the extension

You ask for access, you get the file and you load it in chrome://extensions. You open Vinted with the account you already use. Stoqzy uses that session, just like you do when you browse.

You fill in what only you know

Cost, SKU, where it's stored, how many units you have. It's ten minutes of work with the CSV, and it's what turns “price” into “margin”.

You switch on what you want, and it waits its turn

Restocker, price drops, accepting offers. Every action joins the queue and comes out with a gap and some variation, inside the active hours you set. If Vinted shows a block, it stops and tells you.

03 · Safety and pace

Your account is worth more than the rush.

This is why Stoqzy exists. Read both columns: what it does and what it refuses to do count for just as much, and none of these rules can be switched off.

What it does

  • SessionIt runs in your browser, with your session. There is no server of ours talking to Vinted. The calls leave from your own page, as if you were the one clicking.
  • PaceA human pace, and it's compulsory. Caps per hour and per day, a pause between actions with random variation, and active hours you set yourself (9am to 11pm out of the box). Outside them the queue waits.
  • ProfilesSeveral accounts on the same computer don't wake up at the same time. Each profile starts its active hours with its own fixed delay of up to 45 minutes. Dozens of accounts relisting at 09:00 on the dot from the same IP don't look like people.
  • CaptchaIt stops at the first block and tells you. The queue stays still until you decide.
  • DuplicatesRelisting creates the new one and hides the old one in the same operation. Two open tabs don't create two listings: only one of them can claim each task.
  • DataRecords, costs and settings stay in your Chrome. You export the lot to CSV whenever you want.
  • Currency21 Vinted countries, with each item's price handled in that item's own currency.
  • When it changesIf Vinted changes the API, the extension spots the drift and switches off the affected action instead of writing blind.

What it refuses to do

  • It doesn't solve captchas, it doesn't get around anti-bot protection, it doesn't change IP. If Vinted says no, then it's no.
  • It doesn't ask for your password, doesn't see it and doesn't store it.
  • It doesn't fire messages one after another. Each message to an interested buyer takes a click from you, and it joins the queue.
  • There's no “fast mode” button. The pace caps can't be switched off.
  • It doesn't delete listings. It only does what we've already confirmed in real traffic, and deleting isn't confirmed yet; relisting hides the old one.
  • It doesn't restock everything you've ever sold. Restocking sold items has a cut-off date: only the sales from the day you switch it on.
  • It doesn't promise that Vinted will never restrict you. What it does promise is that, if that happens, it won't have been a burst of ours.

The three modes (Cautious, Balanced, Active) change the caps per day and per hour, the distance between actions and the rest between bursts; the maximum in the code (60 a day, 12 an hour) and the stop at a captcha are always there.

04 · Stoqzy Studio · Team plan

When there are a lot of accounts, you need a workbench.

The extension handles one account at a time. The Studio is the panel where you see them all: balance, sales and stock per account, the shipping queue with custom labels, product templates to list across several accounts, and AI-suggested replies that you approve before sending. It runs at studio.stoqzy.com: every customer has their own API key and their data stays isolated from everyone else's.

  • aAccounts side by sideAvailable and pending balance, sales from the last 30 days and stock for each account, in one table. In euros and in pounds, each one in its own currency.
  • bShipping in batchesThe labels come out with the name you choose (SKU, title or tracking), on thermal 100 × 150 mm or another format, together in one PDF. The ones older than 48 h come first.
  • cProduct templatesOne template, several accounts. You pick which accounts to list on and the Studio keeps the queue, with the Vinted taxonomy (category, size, condition, colours) already in the record. Automatic listing by each account's extension is in testing on our own account and gets switched on when it passes; until then, the template opens in the extension's New listing screen and goes out at the normal pace.
  • dAI replies, approved by youIn the conversation, a button asks for a suggested reply with the item record as context, in the account's language. You read it, correct it if you want, and you send it yourself. Each suggestion costs 1 credit; with no credits, it warns you and spends nothing.

Labels carry buyers' addresses. That's why every customer signs in with their own key, sees only their own accounts, and the PDFs are generated on the spot and never stored. We don't sell or share this data with anyone.

06 · Questions

What people usually ask before installing.

If yours isn't here, write to ola@stoqzy.com. The person who wrote the code answers.

Is it safe for my account?

That's the reason Stoqzy exists. Every write goes through caps per hour and per day, a gap with variation and active hours, and at the first sign of a block or a captcha the queue stops and tells you. There is no “fast” mode. Even so, no tool can promise that an account will never be restricted: what we promise is to do nothing that a careful person wouldn't do.

I use browser profiles (GoLogin, Multilogin) for several accounts. Does it work?

Yes, that's how the author runs his own accounts: one profile per account and the extension installed in each one. The extension only talks to the account open in that profile, and each profile starts its active hours with its own delay, so they don't all wake up in the same minute. To see every account in a single panel there's the Studio, on the Team plan.

Do you need my Vinted password?

No. The extension runs in your browser and uses the session you already have open. It never asks for your password, never sees it and never stores it.

Does relisting duplicate my listings?

No. Relisting creates a new listing and hides the old one in the same operation; the record, the costs and the history move across to the new listing. A sold item goes back up as soon as the sale shows up (or only once it's completed, if you prefer). Vinted doesn't relist the item from a cancelled sale on its own, and the cancellation needs you to accept it, so the duplicate never appears without you knowing.

Do I lose the favourites and the views when I relist?

Yes. The new listing starts from zero, and that's exactly why it goes back to the top of the searches. That's why the Restocker only relists what has been stale for more days than you set (45 by default); whatever is selling stays put.

Does Vinted recognise a repeated photo?

It might. That's why each relist goes out with one of 20 editing profiles it hasn't used on that item yet, and when the profiles run out the extension warns you instead of repeating one. On Studio accounts we recommend using your own photos on each account.

Does it work in my country?

Yes, in all 21 Vinted markets, with the right currency for each item. The United Kingdom, Ireland, Spain, France, Germany, Italy, Portugal, Poland and the rest.

Where does my data live?

The records, costs and settings stay in your browser's storage, and you export the lot to CSV whenever you want. On the Team plan, the sales, the balance and the labels of the connected accounts move to the Studio, at studio.stoqzy.com, isolated by customer key; only your browser keeps talking to Vinted.

Is it on the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. You ask for access by email, you get the extension file and you load it in chrome://extensions. We'll tell you when it's published.

How do I get the updates?

By email, with the new file. You swap the extension folder for the new one and click “Reload” in chrome://extensions. The records, the costs and the settings stay where they were.

How do I pay for Pro and Team?

We agree it by email when you ask for the plan, month by month. The site stores no cards and no payment details.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. The plans are monthly and with no lock-in; while the billing is agreed by email, you cancel with an email. When you cancel, you go back to free and you carry on seeing the wardrobe, the sales and the margin.

And what doesn't it do yet?

It doesn't delete listings (we haven't confirmed that write in real traffic yet), it doesn't get around captchas or anti-bot protection, and it doesn't do your accounting. Whatever isn't confirmed yet is marked “not enabled” inside the extension, instead of us pretending it works. Every change goes through 1,900 automated tests before it reaches you.
